Draw to Smash!

"Draw to Smash!" is a creative physics logic puzzle game where your drawings come to life. Sketch falling weights, ramps, and crushing boulders to smash mischievous eggs.

Description  Draw to Smash!

  • Freeform Sketching Engine: Draw any line, polygon, or lever and watch realistic gravity take over immediately.
  • Humorous Smashing Mechanics: Crush expressive bad eggs hiding behind shields, shelves, and swinging cages.
  • Unlimited Creative Solutions: Every stage can be cleared in multiple ingenious ways depending on your sketches.
  • Hundreds of Brain-Teasers: Puzzles introduce portals, spikes, TNT barrels, and moving obstacles.

How to Play:

  • Draw Shape: Click and drag mouse anywhere on canvas to sketch your line or heavy shape.
  • Gravity Drop: Release mouse to materialize the object and let physics crash it down onto eggs.
  • Protect Innocents: Ensure you only smash the villainous eggs while sparing protected characters.
  • Use Single Stroke: Clear the stage using minimal ink to earn maximum 3-star ratings.
