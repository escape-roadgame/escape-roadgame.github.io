Truck Loader 4

"Truck Loader 4" elevates the puzzle platformer franchise with modern warehouse challenges, refined physics, and ingenious obstacles. Take the wheel of your trusty robotic crane truck and fulfill demanding logistics contracts.

Description  Truck Loader 4

  • Modernized Puzzle Gameplay: Tackle fresh mechanical puzzles with advanced switches, moving barriers, and dynamic physics objects.
  • High-Torque Magnetic Crane: Seamlessly lift, toss, and stack heavy metallic boxes with ultra-responsive physics feedback.
  • Diverse Cargo Varieties: Carefully handle volatile hazard crates, dense steel boxes, and delicate fragile packages.
  • Clever Stage Design: Every level introduces unique problem-solving twists that reward smart thinking and timing.
  • Instant Browser Play: Smooth 60 FPS gameplay with zero installation required, perfect for school and home.

How to Play:

  • Drive & Position: Use A and D (or Left/Right Arrows) to steer the mini loader truck across the platform.
  • Extend the Arm: Aim the crane arm in 360 degrees using your mouse pointer.
  • Activate the Magnet: Click and hold the Left Mouse Button to lock onto crates and interactive metal objects.
  • Trigger Environmental Mechanics: Place boxes on pressure plates, press buttons with the arm, and clear blocked pathways.
  • Stack Inside the Hauler: Place all required cargo boxes squarely inside the green loading boundaries to finish the stage.
