Paint Blob Ball

"Paint Blob Ball" is a vibrant, satisfying maze puzzle game. Roll a squishy paint ball through clean labyrinth corridors, splashing bright paint until every square inch is colored.

Description  Paint Blob Ball

  • Satisfying Paint Splatters: Watch dull white corridors coat with glossy, vivid liquid colors.
  • Sliding Momentum Mechanics: The ball glides continuously until hitting a solid barrier.
  • Hundreds of Maze Layouts: Tackle clever labyrinths requiring deliberate navigational routes.
  • Relaxing Haptic Feedback: Enjoy smooth animations and clean, stress-free puzzle mechanics.

How to Play:

  • Swipe to Roll: Use Arrow keys, WASD, or mouse swipe to launch the paint ball in any direction.
  • Slide to Wall: The ball rolls in a straight line until it strikes an opposing wall.
  • Cover Every Tile: Navigate corners strategically so no unpainted white spots remain.
  • Complete Level: Covering 100% of the maze surface instantly unlocks the next colorful level.
