Dunkbrush

"Dunkbrush" is a creative physics puzzle sports game. Draw ink ramps and slides on screen to direct falling basketballs into hoops while keeping dangerous bombs away.

Description  Dunkbrush

  • Draw-To-Score Physics: Sketch custom ramp lines that deflect basketballs smoothly into hoops.
  • Hazardous Falling Bombs: Divert explosive bombs away from the net before they detonate.
  • Limited Ink Reserve: Manage your brush ink meter carefully on each dropping wave.
  • Collectable Brushes & Balls: Unlock colorful crayons, neon markers, and patterned basketballs.

How to Play:

  • Draw Lines: Click and drag your mouse to paint ramps in mid-air.
  • Guide Basketballs: Form angles that bounce descending balls cleanly into the hoop.
  • Deflect Hazards: Draw protective roofs over the hoop to deflect falling bombs off-screen.
  • Manage Ink: Watch your ink percentage bar; conserve strokes for critical deflection angles.
